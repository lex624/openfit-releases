# OpenFit privacy policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 (updated the same day to disclose MediaPipe). Developer: Misty Addai.
Privacy/support contact: j.more.ljb@gmail.com. Public policy URL: https://github.com/lex624/openfit-releases/blob/main/PRIVACY.md.

OpenFit is a fitness and wellbeing application. It is not a medical device and
does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. Consult a qualified
healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Measurements
and derived trends are not guaranteed to be accurate or complete.

## Information accessed and why

With your permission, OpenFit reads selected Health Connect activity, sleep,
physiological and body measurements to show dashboards and trends and determine
movement reminders. It can read/write blood-pressure records and mindfulness
sessions through their user controls. Background health reads support reminders.
You choose the permissions, and declining optional access limits the corresponding
feature rather than granting broader access automatically.

You may enter food, body, pressure, exercise and journal information or import
health documents. Those records and cached measurements are kept in this
installation's private app storage. Bluetooth access supports compatible sensors
and optional Fitbit Air wrist alerts. The Air integration is independent and
unofficial, not endorsed by Google or Fitbit. User-started microphone analysis
classifies snore-like sound on-device and retains analysis results, not audio.

## External services

If you connect Google Health, OpenFit exchanges the authorised connection and
data requests with Google. Optional AI chat requires a separate health-sharing
choice. When you consent and ask a question, your question, conversation and
current/archived health context are sent to OpenAI using your configured account
or API key. Provider terms, retention and deletion practices apply; turning off
future sharing does not retract a request already transmitted.

Document recognition uses Google ML Kit. Recognition inputs and results are
processed locally. Google documents separate SDK collection of device/app
information and performance/usage metrics, and possible update requests. OpenFit has no developer-operated health-data server or advertising
service.

Snore tracking uses Google's MediaPipe library on your device. MediaPipe sends
Google usage statistics, such as app and device details and performance
information. It does not send audio or health data.

## Storage, retention and choices

Local records remain until you remove them using the relevant feature or clear
the installation's data. Credentials and health caches have additional secure
storage protection. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for
this version's app data. Exports you create are separate files under your control
and may be copied or backed up by other apps you use.

Revoke permissions through Android Settings and Health Connect. Disconnecting
a source removes its local connection/cache, not all manual logs, documents or
source records. Android Settings → Apps → OpenFit → Storage → Clear storage, or
uninstalling, removes this installation's local app data. Manage exported files,
Health Connect records and data already sent to external providers separately.
The developer does not create or delete your Google/OpenAI accounts. Contact
j.more.ljb@gmail.com for questions or requests about developer-controlled processing.

## Audience, security and changes

OpenFit is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
Security depends on the device and configured services; no system provides an
absolute guarantee. Network-provider exchanges use HTTPS; Bluetooth wrist
commands use the band transport. This policy will be updated when OpenFit's data practices change.
