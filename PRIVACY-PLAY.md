# OpenFit privacy policy (Google Play edition)

Effective date: 25 November 2026. Developer: Misty Addai.
Privacy/support contact: j.more.ljb@gmail.com. Public policy URL: https://github.com/lex624/openfit-releases/blob/main/PRIVACY-PLAY.md.

OpenFit is a fitness and wellbeing application. It is not a medical device and
does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. Consult a qualified
healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Measurements
and derived trends are not guaranteed to be accurate or complete.

## Information accessed and why

With your permission, OpenFit reads selected Health Connect activity, sleep,
heart, physiological and body measurements to show your dashboard and trends and
to decide whether to send a movement reminder. It can read and write
blood-pressure records you enter and mindfulness sessions you complete. Background
health reads are used only for movement-reminder checks. You choose the
permissions; declining one limits the matching feature.

You may enter food, body, blood-pressure and exercise information. Those records
and cached measurements are kept in this installation's private app storage.

Optional Bluetooth access connects to standard Bluetooth heart-rate sensors to
show live heart rate. Optional microphone access is used only when you start snore
tracking: sound is classified on the device and only per-minute results are kept,
never audio.

## Information shared

OpenFit has no developer-operated server, account system, advertising or
analytics. Your health data is not sent off your device by OpenFit, sold or
shared with third parties. Data you choose to export goes where you send it.

## Storage, retention and choices

Local records remain until you remove them in the app or clear the app's data.
The health cache is encrypted with a key kept in Android's secure storage.
Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for OpenFit's data.

Revoke permissions in Android Settings and Health Connect. Disconnecting in
OpenFit removes the Health Connect connection and local cache, but not your manual
logs or the records other apps wrote to Health Connect. Android Settings → Apps →
OpenFit → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling, removes all of OpenFit's local
data. Exports and Health Connect records are managed separately.

## Health Connect

OpenFit's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health
Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Audience, security and changes

OpenFit is intended for adults and is not directed at children. No system can
guarantee absolute security. This policy will be updated when OpenFit's data
practices change. Questions: j.more.ljb@gmail.com.
